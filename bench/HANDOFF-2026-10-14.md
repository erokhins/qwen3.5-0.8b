## 5. Traps that bit us — check these before believing a result

1. **A wrapper that sets a flag the caller also sets is a silent precedence fight.** `gate.py` appends `--gemm`
   after the wrapper's and the parser is last-wins → the famous "16/16 both routes" was sixteen hand runs.
   Route/form/scan are now passed only by `engine/gate.py`.
2. **A dry-run stand-in that echoes the value its own checker expects cannot fail.** Fixed for the route echo; still
   worth hunting. Three such checkers were found this session.
3. **Missing dumps masquerade as numerics diffs.** `cmp` against an absent/empty file prints DIFF. **Read the row
   count first, never the diff list** — this happened twice (§17 and route A).
4. **`--negative` that passes must be shown to localise**, not just to fail. A control that catches everything proves
   nothing (there's an `AGENTS.md` entry on it; two more near-misses this session).
5. **LDS overrun doesn't fault** and silently corrupts neighbours: the tiled attention kernel wrote 141 KB past a
   4 KB staging array. Check LDS budget explicitly on every new kernel; `hipcc -Wall` **unused variable** warnings
   have each cost a factor of >10 in one kernel or another.
6. **Never trust a cost model over the counter pass.** Phase 0 priced ≥1.5× for the chunked scan; the device returned
   **5.9× slower**. Pre-registered win lines are the only defence — keep using them.
