### Q4b — margin calibration from legal routes only (**D-3**, gates entry to Q5)
The margin must stop being a ratio fit to the engine's own errors. Add a **third legal route variant**
(e.g. a different GDN chunk size under §04.7, or a different but equally legal summation order) and
compute `route(a,b)` vs `route(a,c)`: the ratio between two independent legal spreads is a multiplier that
does not reference the engine. Report it, compare against the fitted 3.50 / per-fixture values, and if the
engine-independent multiplier is smaller than what the engine needs, **that is a finding about the engine,
not a licence to loosen** — report and stop.

*Acceptance:* a documented, engine-independent multiplier in `engine/results.md`, the per-fixture margins
re-derived or explicitly kept with the reason, and every fixture still green.

**Done 2026-10-12 — the answer is KEEP, and the multiplier is 1.19–1.96.** The third legal route is a different
GDN chunk width under §04.7 (legal by §04.7 verbatim, gated by `run_chunkcheck.py` / G3, A-08), priced at several
widths so it cannot be one width's artifact, plus a second orthogonal axis — §07.3's blocked `(max, sum)` decode
softmax — reported but deliberately excluded from the conservative multiplier because the shipped engine does
not take that freedom. The oracle was not edited (its sha256 is pinned into every allowance file); the width is
rebound in-process only and `--check-patch` proves the rebind bit-identical to route a at the shipped width.
Outcome per fixture: the engine needs 3.04 / 3.49 / 7.98 / 2.92 where the widest legal route reaches 1.80 / 1.72
/ 1.96 / 1.54, so **the trap fired on all four** and the instruction "report and stop" is what was done — no band
touched, no fixture re-priced, every fixture's green state as recorded under D-2. Two findings came out of it
that matter more than the number: the margin's numerator is dominated by an **oracle-vs-dump** term (specimpl's
own route a needs 1.94–3.78 in margin units against the same dump, and on `dec64f` more than the engine does),
which no route pair inside the oracle can produce — and the shipped `margin_rel` divides a ratio of means by a
mean of ratios, so it is not a like-for-like constant. Write-up, tables and the one rule shape that could still
make this engine-independent (an absolute legal envelope per step, against the pin): `engine/results.md`, the
D-3 section. Instrument: `engine/route_margin.py` (CPU-only, read-only over `data/refs`, 12-case `--selftest`).

### Q6 — Tier 3: single-stream performance, in this order
1. **Full-plan burst experiment** — 424 launches in dependency order, one sync at the end, compared to the
   engine's 11.919 ms. Decides which family the ~2.5 ms residue is in: serialized dispatch (≈237 launches
   × ~10 µs ≈ the residue) vs per-step sync + host work. Cheapest number that changes strategy.
2. **Launch-count reduction** — fold the 115 dtype casts into producers/consumers (48 of them are the MLP
   staging), widen block widths (`glue` 512 t ≫ 256/128), consider one fused small-op kernel per layer.
3. **Block-parallel attention for 4k** — mandatory at the chosen context target; 2.014 µs/lane extrapolates
   to ~50 tok/s at 4k. Price it first against a measured curve at 64/256/512/1024 lanes, not against the
   single slope.
4. **Narrow-shape projections** — `k_proj`/`v_proj` 40 %, `o_proj`/`out_proj` ~49 %, `in_proj_a`/`in_proj_b`
   **5 %**; this is what caps the family at 63–64 %. Shape problem, not tile problem.
5. **`proj_gemv --only gdn|attn` selector** + one clean timing run each, to fill ledger §2's two
   "not measured on their own" rows (they cannot be filled arithmetically — best-per-shape sums to 3.54 ms
   against the shipped plan's 3.081 ms).

A key is (file, key), so per-fixture margins are per-fixture keys and **one fixture's record can never excuse another's**; that is D-2 expressed in the ledger, and a single global margin key would have defeated the whole exercise. Moving a band now takes a line, and `record` refuses to write one whose reason is empty, thin once band-mover words are stripped ("loosen", "tune the margin", "adjust the band"), pass-seeking ("so it passes", "needed to pass", "for now", "temporarily"), or whose decision is a `D-x` that is not in the log above — because "we needed it to pass" is a motive and not a justification. `record` also refuses a line whose `--new` is not what the tree holds, so the ledger cannot drift from the code it describes. Where the value is generated, the generator writes the line: `engine/route_allowance.py` appends its own margins when it prices a fixture **and logs a refusal** (`event: refused`) when a re-pricing disagrees with the shipped margin — an attempted loosening that was caught is the most useful thing in the log, and it moves no band. Deleting a key counts as moving it: dropping a fixture's `route_allowance.json` (which hands it back to the flat floors) fails the check, while a fixture directory that is simply not installed on that box is skipped out loud, because `data/` is gitignored and an absent artifact is not a decision.

What a determined author can still do, stated plainly, because a police that overstates itself is worse than none. They can edit `tools/band_history.py` itself — the parser, the key set, or the boilerplate list — and **the checker is not covered by its own history**; that is unavoidable self-reference, and the backstop is code review plus the `review` git-walk mode this tool still carries, not the tool. They can write a fluent false `reason`: the checker judges whether a reason has content, not whether it is true, and only a reviewer who knows what `dec300f` step 86 measured can catch a confident lie. They can rewrite the ledger — it is append-only by convention, and a rewrite is visible in that file's own `git log -p`, not to `check`. They can bypass the gate entirely (`engine/build/decode_run` runs without asking), since what is policed is the *gate*, not the device. And the per-step route spreads inside an allowance file are **not** ledger keys: they are held by `gate.py:allowance_problems` (each cap must equal the file's margin times that step's spread, the margin must admit the engine deficits recorded in the same file, and the prompt/feed/specimpl hashes must still match), which catches a hand-edited cap but would be satisfied by an edit that changed a spread and its cap together — if that ever needs closing, the shape of the fix is one digest key per fixture, recorded whenever `route_allowance.py` writes.


## 4. Standing rules that bind every item

- **One subagent at a time.** Parallelism buys nothing beyond the token-sharing penalty: the operator
  reports the serving engine slows **~2× with two concurrent requests**, and every agent on this box is
  served by that single engine — so a second worker does not double throughput, it halves both. Big items
  are therefore split into a **planning worker followed by sequential execution workers** (see Q5) rather
  than fanned out.
- **The main agent owns every timing** and every number that enters
  `bench/performance-status.md`; workers may run correctness-only device checks **under ~60 s** and must
  report command + wall time + `util` and say "no timing taken".
- **Never loosen a gate to make it pass.** Bands are derived from measured floors; a new constant needs a
  floor probe.
- **Device measurements blocking, zero subagents**, spread stated, `util` discounted; do not hold
  `gpu_lock.sh` when running `glue` / `proj_gemv` / `gdn_gated_norm` — they self-lock and will park.
- **Never write chat-template special tokens literally** anywhere — refer to them by id (248046 eos;
  248068/248069 think-boundary). This has truncated agent messages and a worker brief.
- **Deep-fixture prompt rule**: the prompt must actually generate many tokens — pre-flight with
  `tools/stop_probe.py`, require no stop id across the whole intended generation (≥128 target, ≥80 before
  pinning), and record the first-stop index in the manifest. Known-good, no stop within 120: count-1-to-80
  (`dec64t`), 200-word Paris paragraph, months-and-lengths list, bicycle explanation, prime continuation.
- **Pin with teacher forcing for depth** — free-run id parity beyond ~8 tokens is A-18 territory and will
  fail for correct code.
- Commit by topic with explicit pathspecs; commit WIP rather than lose it.
