  ALL-IN  (FEED + DRAIN + the shared stage round, /touches)=   2.10 slots/touch incl. staging and both barriers

  CONTROL, both spellings (same 16x16 geometry, shipped VALU dot+fold, no WMMA):
    m16_scalar        63.13 slots/touch — shipped publish shape (only lane 0 stores); 5.00 lane-movement ops/touch, the shipped fold shape
    m16_scalar_ob     59.86 slots/touch — every lane's folded value stored; 5.00 lane-movement ops/touch, the shipped fold shape
  -> CONTROL IN THE VERDICT: 59.86 slots/touch (the observable spelling, and the smaller of the two).
     Against the shipped M = 1 touch of 45, that is the RE-TILE TAX ALONE: +14.86 slots/touch
     on the VALU path, before any instruction class changes. It is the number `prototype/attn_m2`
     measured on device (7-10 % slower at short widths, 16-32 blocks) and it is the debit the
     WMMA credit has to clear.

  VERDICT LINE. A WMMA pass-1 touch is FEED + DRAIN: the score round and store are inside DRAIN,
  and at M = 16 there is no per-touch loop left to bill — the loop is 16 K-steps per 256 touches.
  The two ways to price the residue are printed because D57's own bottom-up assumed it survives:
    residue = 45 - (dot 16 + fold 11) = 18, as D57 priced it                19.73 slots/touch -> BETWEEN THE LINES
    residue = 0, measured: no per-touch loop survives the tile (m16_bf16rt - m16_empty)   1.73 slots/touch -> FUNDABLE
  (gate lines: <= 19 fund, >= 30 dead; in between is an answer too)

## 4b. The matrix pipe, which is not an issue slot
  one v_wmma_f32_16x16x16 (wave32) = 128 MACs/lane = 32 clk at the WMMA roof, 36.0 clk at the measured sustained 3.552 MAC/lane/clk
  so the SCORE tile's pipe time is 16 x 36.0 = 577 clk per warp per round = 2.25 clk/touch — i.e. the matrix pipe, not the
  feed, is the floor: 2.25 clk/touch replaces 45 issue slots/touch, and the feed+drain (1.73) can be hidden behind it only if other wavefronts
  are resident to issue while the pipe is busy — which is an OCCUPANCY question, §4c.
  The pipe is per-SIMD and 4 warps share a block across 2 SIMDs, so a
  block resident alone on a CU puts ~2 of its 4 warps per SIMD: enough to overlap one warp's WMMA
  with another's feed only if the block count is not itself driven to 1 by VGPR or LDS. That is
  the term §1's wf/CU column carries, and it is why the verdict is printed per variant in §3c.

## 4d. The traffic a global-A spelling buys and costs, as arithmetic on the probe's own geometry
  A read from global: 1 global load(s) in the rolled K-step body, so A is re-read EVERY K-step,
  not once per round: 16 x 512 B = 8192 B per warp per round = 32 B/touch.
  K read from global: 8192 B per block per round, shared by 4 warps x 256 touches = 8 B/touch — against the shipped 4096 B for 32 touches = 128 B/touch.
  -> the tile cuts global K traffic 16x (one sub-block feeds MROWS rows instead of one), and buys it with 32 B/touch of A re-reads.
  Whether those re-reads are cheap is an L2 question, not a slot question: the 16 rows are 4 KiB
  apart (GQ_LDM = ROW_H), so each 16 B lane load lands in a different line and the lines fill out
  only across the round's 16 K-steps. This compile-only census cannot settle it; it is the first
  thing the device pass must, and §5's answer depends on it.

## 5. The resource-conditioned answer, derived rather than asserted
  variants holding >= 4 blocks/CU: m16_bf16gq (1.98 slots/touch, 20 wf/CU)
  -> the WMMA spellings that hold >= 4 blocks/CU: m16_bf16gq; cheapest is m16_bf16gq at 1.98 slots/touch, 5 blocks / 20 wf/CU (30 VGPR, 11264 B), bound by LDS.
     Its VGPR is low ONLY because the K loop is rolled (§1b): the same tile unrolled is 235 VGPR at 1 block(s)/CU. Register pressure, not the feeder, is what the M = 16 tile
     costs, and that is the same wall D30 §2 priced for the PV leg's accumulators: the score leg alone,
     unrolled, already spends more than half the 512-register SIMD budget before PV is mentioned.
  the PV N-split, witnessed by the score leg: 16 f32 accumulators over DH = 256 is 128 VGPR/lane (D30 §2).
     This probe's SCORE leg alone needs 235 VGPR unrolled and 28 rolled. A block that holds both legs unrolled cannot
     exist under the 512-register budget, so the N-split is a precondition of any port, not an optimisation.
  LDS at or under the shipped 12288 B: m16_bf16gq (11264 B, 30 VGPR, 1.98 slots/touch)
  — that set is the answer to 'is the low-LDS spelling also the low-VGPR one', read rather than argued.

## 6. The cost side, from measured rows only — a model with named inputs, not a measurement
  inputs, every one measured somewhere else in this tree:
    whole-kernel bill        89.9 warp-inst/touch  (bench/performance-status.md §26 W2, counter-calibrated)
    shipped pass-1 window    45 slots/touch     (prototype/attn_valu_slim/results.md §2; --calib re-reads it)
    slot->time elasticity    time ∝ slots^0.39–0.44      (D21/D22; the exponent prototype/attn_m2/results.md §8.4 also used)
    attn / wave              264.71 ms / 763.49 ms   (bench/performance-status.md §25 and §26; attn = 34.7 % of the wave)
    matrix headroom          ~36 µs of matrix work per wave against 257 ms of attn (D34): the
                             matrix PIPE is never the constraint here; only the geometry tax can be
  the occupiable spelling is m16_bf16gq: the score leg goes 45 -> 1.98 issue slots/touch plus 2.25
  clk/touch of matrix pipe, so the whole-kernel bill is 89.9 -> 46.9 (slots only) or 49.1
  if the matrix pipe is charged as an issue-equivalent (the conservative reading).
  elasticity transfer: attn x 0.751..0.790, i.e. -24.9 % to -21.0 % BEFORE any geometry tax
  geometry tax measured at this class by m2 at short widths (x1.07): attn -19.7 % to -15.5 %  ->  wave -6.8 % to -5.4 % of its time  ->  prefill +5.7 % to +7.3 % tok/s
  geometry tax priced by m2 at the funded ruler (x1.29): attn -3.1 % to +1.9 %  ->  wave -1.1 % to +0.7 % of its time  ->  prefill -0.7 % to +1.1 % tok/s
  D30's kill line is that the route must beat `attn` by more than ~15 %. Read this way the band
  STRADDLES it: it clears on m2's measured tax and fails on m2's priced one. The gate question
  (slots per touch) answers decisively; the funding question turns on a geometry tax that is a
  device number, and this compile-only census does not answer it. What would is the device pass
  handed over beside this census (paired A/B, one lock hold, behavioural canary first), and the
  two things it must settle are the A-from-global traffic and the tile-local score staging's effect
  on pass 2 — neither of which is a slot question.
