READ OUT: position 1 accepts at 0.75-0.83 on real prose and every further position is worth less
(0.52-0.60, then 0.42-0.46, then 0.27-0.33), which is why the speedup peaks at n_max=2 and is already
falling by n_max=4. Our engine's own corpus accept rate for the same first position is 0.6556
(`prototype/mtp_accept/M3-RESULT.md` §2) — same ballpark, different prompt/response pair, and llama.cpp's
responses here are chat-template thinking prose (see the doc's caveats), so this ladder is NOT a
claim that llama.cpp drafts better than we do.
