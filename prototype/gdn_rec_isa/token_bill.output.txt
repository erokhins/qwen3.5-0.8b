SECTION 2  the harness TU (`SAVE_TEMPS=1 ./prototype/gdn_rec_isa/build.sh`), which carries the new MODE:
  command: .venv/bin/python prototype/gdn_rec_isa/token_bill.py --asm tmp/gdn_isa/gdn_rec_isa.s \
             --kernels rec2c4_kernel,recloop_kernel,recptr_kernel --selftest
  reading: recptr is -4 flat / +9 per-token against recloop, and its address-arithmetic count is HIGHER
  (+11), because six 64-bit pointer steps cost more slots than the multiply chain they replace.
  The two looped forms' PER-TOKEN lines differ (1327 vs 1336); that difference is the behavioural canary
  `run_screen_recptr.sh` asserts before any device window is spent, because a stale binary would print the
  same bill on both sides and read as a clean null.
