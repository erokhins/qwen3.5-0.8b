# Route (c) audit — 2026-10-14 — the hand↔library GEMM delta is reassociation, not a bug

**VERDICT — one line:** every hand↔library difference this audit can see is reassociation rounding:
isolated, each of the ten library shapes disagrees with hipBLAS on **at most 1 ULP of any element
above the cancellation band** (0 for three shapes at M=1) with `meanrel ≤ 3.9e-5`, `cos ≥
0.99999991`, the whole-tree difference is **≤ 1.9e-3 (1-cos) per logits row** and flips **0 of 1566
greedy decisions that had ≥ 0.10 of top-1/top-2 room** while flipping 24/128 that had < 0.10, and
**every one of the three injected bugs clears the legal delta by ≥ 492× on the least flattering
statistic** — so the audit's statistics separate a real bug from this delta by ~500–10⁸×, and the
`--gemm library` route needs no allowance beyond what §10 already grants.

Plan: `progress/2026-10-13/plan-route-c-audit-2026-10-13.md`. Nothing here touched `engine/gate.py`,
`engine/route_allowance.py`, `tools/band_history.py`, a pin, or the `--gemm` default.

Dump and report artifacts live under `tmp/` (gitignored, as always) and are regenerated by the
commands in §0; the two tools that produce and read them are committed.

**Two places where the plan's pre-registered band does not survive contact with the engine.** Both
are reported rather than quietly re-banded, and both are decisions for the parent, not for me:

1. **The A1 floor is a per-matmul floor; A2 and A3 measure a 24-layer cascade.** Applied literally
   ("no element beyond its own shape's A1 max ULP" at the logits; "any stage beyond 2× its floor"
   for stages), every fixture and every stage fails — by 3–5 orders of magnitude on the raw max
   ULP. That is not a bug finding, it is the band being applied one whole network downstream of
   where it was measured: A1 says one matmul moves a large element by ≤ 1 ULP, and a BF16 element
   only *exists* to 0.4 % (2⁻⁸), so after 24 layers of residual adds a reassociated matmul has
   decorrelated the cascade to meanrel ~10⁻² and sign flips at large magnitude. §A2/§A3 below show
   the discriminator the plan itself names (locality) pointing the other way, and §A4 shows what a
   bug looks like next to it. A usable cascade band would be `meanrel ≤ 3e-2` / `cos ≥ 0.9998` per
   stage and `1-cos ≤ 2e-3` per logits row, all of which everything measured here satisfies with
   margin; **pricing that band into a gate is the parent's call** (it belongs in `engine/gate.py` /
   `route_allowance.py`, both out of scope here).
2. **The engine has no per-layer stage-dump facility** — the plan's "one fixed step, every stage
   tensor" premise is only partly satisfiable without an engine change I was not authorised to make.
   `route_c_stages.cpp` snapshots the `DecodeScratch` tiles *after a whole body*, so each tile
   holds its **last** writer inside that body (layer 23 for the attention and MLP tiles, layer 22
   for the GDN ones), not each layer's occurrence. §A3 states the consequence.

## A1 — isolated per-shape reassociation floor: **1 ULP, at every width, for all ten shapes**

Ten library shapes (`tools/prefill_gemm_shapes.py`) × M ∈ {1, 88, 512, 1024}, **real weights**
(from the resident checkpoint, `resident_mib=1474.1`) and **real activations** (1024 rows of the
shipped fixtures' own rendered prompts, through the real chat template and the real `norm` — not a
synthetic fill). Plus `gate_up_stage`, the MLP act as the fused hand kernel and the unfused
library path each produce it.

The metric, once, because it is what the whole audit turns on: a **raw** max-ULP on a dot is
meaningless (§10.1 F1–F4, `prototype/bf16_ulp.py`'s own docstring) — a legal reassociation moves
near-zero cancelled elements by any number of ULP, up to a sign flip. So each shape is reported
twice: `maxULP` raw, and `maxULPlarge` = the maximum over elements with `|ref| > 1e-4·max|ref|`.
`>1ULP_large` counts the large elements that moved by more than 1 ULP, `n_bad` counts the
band-excluded (cancelled) offenders, and `worst_off_rel` is the rel-magnitude of the worst
offender, i.e. the smallest band under which nothing offends. Full table: `progress/bench/evidence/rc_a1/a1.txt`,
machine-readable `progress/bench/evidence/rc_a1/a1.json`.

| shape | K | M=1 | M=88 | M=512 | M=1024 | `maxULPlarge` | `meanrel` worst | cos worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `q_proj` | 1024 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **1** | 1.0e-6 | 0.999999997 |
| `k_proj` | 1024 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **1** | 1.4e-6 | 0.999999996 |
| `v_proj` | 1024 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **1** | 2.2e-6 | 0.999999996 |
| `o_proj` | 2048 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **1** | 1.5e-5 | 0.999999988 |
| `out_proj` | 2048 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **1** | 1.3e-6 | 0.999999990 |

| `in_proj_z` | 1024 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **1** | 1.2e-6 | 0.999999997 |

Four readings that matter:

- **`>1ULP_large` = 0 for all ten shapes at all four widths.** Not "small" — *zero elements whose
  magnitude is above 1e-4 of the tensor max moved by more than 1 ULP.* Three shapes
  (`q_proj`, `k_proj`, `in_proj_z`) come out **bit-identical at M=1**: when hipBLAS's tile order
  happens to match the hand GEMV's, reassociation does not even differ.
- The raw `maxULP` column ranges to 27 459 (`in_proj_qkv@1024`) and every one of those maxima sits on
  a cancelled element — e.g. `+1.9e-06 → −7.2e-07` at `rel = 4.5e-8` of a max of 42. That is the
  sign-flip-at-zero the floor probe predicted, and the reason a gate banded on raw max-ULP passes
  vacuously or fails vacuously. `n_bad` (offenders inside the band) runs 1–163 out of 10² –10⁶
  elements; `worst_off_rel` ≤ 5.4e-5 for the ten projection shapes.
- **The floor does not grow with M.** `maxULPlarge` is 1 at 88, 512 and 1024 alike, `%diff` is flat
  (0.05 % for the 1024-K projections, 0.18–0.2 % for `o_proj`/`out_proj`, 0.25–0.3 % for
  `down_proj`), and `meanrel` actually *falls* with M for most shapes. Width-locality was the plan's
  test for "a kernel reading past a body boundary"; there is no such trend, and every destination
  was poison-prefilled before each side wrote it, with the survivors checked
  (`poison_survivors=0` on every floor and injection bin in `progress/bench/evidence/rc_a1.log`) — so untouched-but-read
  destinations cannot hide behind a stale buffer.
- **The one exception is the MLP act, and it is an epilogue fact, not a matmul fact.** `gate_proj`
  and `up_proj` individually floor at 1 ULP; the *act* `bf(silu(bf(gate))) · bf(up)` floors at 5–8
  ULP on 290 band-excluded elements of 3.67 M at M=1024 (plus 471 inside the cancellation band),
  `worst_off_rel` 0.186, `meanrel` 2.1e-6, cos 0.99999999. Both routes apply the *same three-rounding*
  epilogue (BF16 gate, BF16 silu output, BF16 product — the hand kernel's comment says so and
  `mlp_silu_mul_kernel` matches), so the only input difference is the ≤1-ULP dot result; three
  chained roundings are not 1-Lipschitz in ULP units, so a 1-ULP nudge on the gate dot can land 5–8
  ULP away on the act when the pre-rounded product sits at a tie. The worst offenders are all
  `rel ≈ 2e-4` of a tensor max of 314 with an absolute difference of 1.5e-3 (`hand −0.060546875 →
  lib −0.0625` at flat 2736931, 8 ULP): small values on a tensor whose max is an outlier. This is
  the one floor a band-writer must carry as >1 ULP, and it belongs to the act stage, not to a dot.

**A1 stop-and-report check:** no shape exceeded its own max; the maxima above are the floors.

**A4 conclusion: PASS** — the audit's statistics are 492×–10⁸× away from being unable to tell a bug
from this delta. The delta is not a bug, by the audit's own pre-registered instrument.

## Verdict, and what it does and does not license

The hand↔library GEMM delta is reassociation rounding:

1. at the site, it is ≤ 1 ULP on every band-excluded element of all ten library shapes at all four
   widths (three shapes bit-identical at M=1), with meanrel ≤ 3.9e-5 and cos ≥ 0.99999991 — an order
   of magnitude inside anything §10 bands;
2. it does not grow with body width, so no kernel is reading past a boundary;
3. accumulated over a whole 24-layer prefill body it reaches meanrel ≤ 1.7e-2 per stage and 1-cos ≤
   1.9e-3 per logits row — inside the flat §10 floors every route-c fixture already passes, and of
   the same size as the library route's known delta against the pins;
4. it flips no greedy decision that had ≥ 0.10 of room in 1694 dumped rows across all eight
   fixtures, and 18.8 % of the ones that had < 0.10 (24 rows, all listed with both gaps);
5. three injected bugs — including the historical stale-tile shape — are caught by every statistic
   by ≥ 492×, so the instrument is not vacuous.

**What this licenses:** treating `--gemm library` as numerically legal for this model, and judging it
with aggregate bands (per-stage `meanrel`/`cos`, per-row `1-cos`, and decision-margin-bucketed token
agreement) rather than with raw max-ULP at the logits.

**What it does not license, and needs a decision rather than a silent choice:**

- pricing the *cascade* band (§0.1) into `engine/gate.py` / `engine/route_allowance.py`. The numbers
  to price from are `progress/bench/evidence/rc_a1/a1.json` (per-shape floors, including the 5–8 ULP act-stage floor that
  a per-dot band would miss) and §A2's table.
- whether route (c) proceeds on the strength of the hand route's own gate status plus this audit, or
  whether the parent wants the library route gated against the pins per fixture first (the gate-side
  question this audit deliberately did not touch: no gate file, allowance, or pin was modified).
- the `gate_up_stage` floor of 5–8 ULP: if a stage-local BF16 gate is ever put on the MLP act, it
  must be written against 8 ULP, not 1 — a 1-ULP gate there would fail on legal data.

Two things worth carrying into the traps table, both discovered while building this:

- a stage-tile snapshot taken after a body silently compares a tile a route **never writes** against
  uninitialised memory (`mlp_gate_raw`/`mlp_up_raw` on the hand side), which reads as a 100 % diff
  with a plausible-looking table row. Name library-only tiles and print them as what they are.
- a `meanrel`-ordered stage list with `embed` in it silently kills a `min(...)` summary line: an
  exactly-identical input stage (meanrel 0) makes every ratio infinite, and the locality line just
  did not print for one run until the zero was excluded.
