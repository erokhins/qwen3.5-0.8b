Take the lock, record util at both ends, stop if util is high and you care about the number:

```bash
tools/gpu_lock.sh acquire review

.venv/bin/python prototype/proj_gemv/run.py --layers all --steps all --negative   # 912/0

```

Then the cold sweeps for the two numbers the ledger needs (`proj_gemv` and `mlp_gemv` cold timing,
`--cold` flags in each `run.py`), and the `rows/block` 32 question on projections (+8 % on a
contended box, worth ~0.2 ms/token, still an open decision).

**Timing caveat, corrected the same day:** `flash_serve` — the server generating these very tokens —
pins the iGPU near 99 % whether or not it is doing anything, and it is not inside the lock. My claim
here that a clean window is therefore unreachable was **wrong**: a *blocking* tool call pauses the
session's own token generation, so measuring synchronously from the turn is clean enough — the three
GEMV families reproduce within ~2 %. See `progress/2026-10-16/performance-status-2026-10-16.md` (frozen ledger §6) for the re-take, and note the part
that is genuinely unstable: launch-bound timings (glue) are bimodal, bandwidth-bound ones are not.
Record `gpu_lock.sh util` anyway; it read 24–91 % across identical clean runs and predicted nothing.

Correctness gates are deterministic on these kernels (no atomics), so they are worth re-running even
under contention; timings are not.

## 5. Known-open — do not mistake these for done

- **G1: `lm_head_gemv` has no per-element rule.** Asserted: argmax, `cos ≥ 0.999`, `meanrel ≤ 0.05`.
  top-5 and `max|d|` printed, not asserted; no floor probe, no negatives, no `bf16_ulp` in that
  directory. A two-row misplacement or a one-term dropped reduction passes today. **The engine's
  logits ship on this gate.** My own attempt to start the fix was stopped mid-run; nothing landed.
- **`prototype/gdn_gated_norm` (§04.5 gated RMSNorm) — written, gated on CPU, never run on a device.**
  The mirror probe passes on dec8 (144 cases, 294 912 elements) and six variants discriminate, which
  closes the missing-assertion half of audit item G2. But its committed headline — "A-01's cast order
  is not what the pins carry, `specimpl.rmsnorm_gated` refused on 105 683 elements" — **is a dtype
  artifact, corrected in `fc444d0`**: the probe handed the oracle an F32-widened weight, which
  speccheck never does. Called the way speccheck calls it (BF16 weight), the oracle is bit-exact,
  **0 of 294 912**. Read `fc444d0` before believing either number. The 74 860-vs-105 683 discrepancy
  recorded there is now explained and reproduced by the probe itself: one A-01 formula, three weight
  dtypes — BF16 0, BF16-rounded-then-widened 74 860, raw F32 checkpoint 105 683, same inputs
  (`prototype/gdn_gated_norm/results.md` §1). The kernel uses `bf16(w)` and was unaffected either way.
- **`engine/**` state and extracted kernels are still uncommitted as of this writing**
  (`state.{h,cpp}`, `kernels{.h,_proj,_gdn,_glue}.cpp`, `state_sizes.py`); the agent is finishing its
  wind-down commit. There is **no `decode_step` yet and no gate harness**, so W5b is not close to
  running, and none of it has ever touched a device.
- **Position 64 is a suspect, not a floor.** The pinned rope dims are reproducible by torch F32 *and*
  glibc `cosf`; the device table is the outlier (2 of 128 entries at p=64, none at the other 276), and
  no ±2-ULP frequency nudge explains it. Leading candidate: our kernel's pre-rope F32 reduction order.
  Needs `z` dumped from the kernel at that position — ledger §5(g), frozen progress/2026-10-16/performance-status-2026-10-16.md:203.
- **G6 / G3-smoke bands encode plumbing.** They passed because speccheck's score matmul silently ran
  F32; a legal rope-policy change turned them red. Fix is to make the score dtype explicit at the call
  site, not to move a band. **G7's corpus floor has ~0.035 of headroom** and one legal change put it
  below its own floor twice — treat a G7 failure at W6 as suspect-the-gate, not suspect-the-kernel.
- **speccheck keeps F32 rope deliberately** (`d06b4e3`). If you see a "fix" for it, that is a
  re-break: the policy is gated in `attn_decode` against the pins, where bit-exactness is attainable.
- **Engine is WIP.** No `decode_step`, no gate harness, nothing device-tested. `prefill`, MTP and the
  first tok/s row have not started.
